Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

The short version: Caipture works entirely on your iPhone. We don't have accounts, we don't run analytics or ads, and we don't collect or receive your photos, camera images or any personal data.

This policy explains how the Caipture app ("Caipture", "the app") handles information. The app is published by Samui Robotics Company Limited ("we", "us").

1. Camera

Caipture uses your camera to show a live preview and to compare your body position with the selected pose. Pose detection runs on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Camera frames are processed in memory and are never stored by us or sent off your device.

2. Photos

  • Photos you take are saved to your device's photo library (with your permission) and to the app's own gallery on your device. We never receive them.
  • Photos you choose as custom templates are processed on your device to detect the pose and remove the background. The result is stored only inside the app on your device.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email, payment card or billing details. The app only checks with Apple whether you have an active purchase.

4. Data stored on your device

The app stores your settings (for example voice guidance and overlay opacity), processed pose data for the built-in poses, the list of photos in the in-app gallery, and the date of your last free PRO try. This information stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

5. What we don't do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs; no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data — we don't collect any.

6. Pose images

The example pose photos in the app are AI-generated and do not depict real people.

7. Children

Caipture is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

8. Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app. If you have a question about your data or rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us below.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
