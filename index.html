Caipture

Strike the pose. Nail the shot.

Caipture overlays a pose on your camera and guides you joint by joint until you match it — then takes the photo for you. Beach, city, landmarks, pools and the slopes.

Everything runs on your iPhone. No accounts, no tracking.
